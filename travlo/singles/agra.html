About Agra

Agra

Nestled on the banks of the Yamuna River in the northern state of Uttar Pradesh, Agra is a city steeped in history, romance, and architectural brilliance. Once the flourishing capital of the mighty Mughal Empire, Agra is home to some of the most iconic monuments in the world—most notably, the Taj Mahal, a white-marble masterpiece and eternal symbol of love. Beyond the Taj, the city boasts the imposing Agra Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site that reflects the grandeur of Mughal architecture, and Fatehpur Sikri, an abandoned red sandstone city that whispers tales of emperors and spiritual quests. But Agra isn’t just about monuments. It's a sensory journey—through bustling markets echoing with the clink of bangles and scent of spices, to the tranquil Mehtab Bagh gardens overlooking the Taj at sunset. From the famed Petha (a sweet delicacy) to the handcrafted leather goods, Agra offers a cultural mosaic that blends the ancient with the vibrant rhythm of everyday life. Whether you're tracing the footsteps of emperors or seeking a quiet moment of reflection by the Yamuna, Agra remains a destination that stirs the soul and lingers in memory.
